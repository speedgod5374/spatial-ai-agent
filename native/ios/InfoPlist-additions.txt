Add this key to the iOS app target's Info.plist:

NSCameraUsageDescription
Spatial Studio uses the camera for on-device ARKit tracking and room-surface detection. Camera frames are not uploaded by this prototype.

Add these keys as well so the installed app can contact a reconstruction worker on the same local network over HTTP during development:

NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription
Spatial Studio connects to your computer on your local network when you choose to reconstruct a room scan.

NSAppTransportSecurity
NSAllowsLocalNetworking = YES
